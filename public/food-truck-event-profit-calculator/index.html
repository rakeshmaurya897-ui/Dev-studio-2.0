03 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before you download.

What does the food truck profit calculator calculate?

It estimates event revenue, variable costs, fixed costs, total costs, net profit, profit margin, break-even orders, break-even revenue and profit per order after variable costs.

Can I use it for a food stall or pop-up?

Yes. You can enter your own event, sales and cost assumptions. It may be adapted to a market stall, festival vendor or pop-up food business.

Does it include menu costing?

Yes. The Menu Costing tab calculates total cost per order, contribution per order and contribution margin from the values you enter.

Does it work in Google Sheets?

The workbook is designed as an .xlsx file for Microsoft Excel and can be imported into Google Sheets. Check formulas and formatting after import.

Does it automatically compare multiple events or currencies?

No. You can change inputs and record separate estimates yourself. The currency code is a label only; the workbook does not perform currency conversion or provide an automated multi-event comparison tool.

Will it guarantee that my event makes a profit?

No. It calculates estimates from your assumptions. Actual sales, costs, weather, footfall, venue rules and other factors can differ.
